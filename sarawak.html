Target: Saradise / Jalan Song Model: Heritage Boutique PoC Currency: USD ($)

Main Success Condition: Leasing a character-rich heritage shophouse in the upscale Saradise lifestyle development or near Jalan Song, capturing S-MM2H retirees and affluent local resident families.

2. Business Positioning

Positioned as: A premium, peaceful scalp treatment sanctuary combining Japanese carbonated water misting, soundproof therapy pods, and micro-camera scalp analytics. A relaxing, multilingual, high-standard hospitality experience.

Avoid being positioned as: A local generic beauty shop, a budget massage center, or a noisy haircut salon.

"Waterfront Scalp Sanctuary. Recharge by the Sarawak River."

Core Customer Promise: Visible improvement in scalp hygiene and deep physical relaxation using premium organic botanicals and Japanese technology.

Market Fit: Sarawak attracts a growing pool of long-term retirees (S-MM2H program) and wealthy resident local families who seek high-end wellness treatments. While Kuching has standard body massage spas and local salons, it lacks dedicated scalp health specialists offering private acoustic pods.

3. Market Context

  • Demographics: Expats concentrated heavily near Jalan Song, Kuching Waterfront, Saradise, and Gala City. Large pool of English/Japanese/Korean speakers.
  • Spending Power: High concentration of wealth due to multinational retirees and business owners. Healthy allocation to leisure and wellness.
  • Environmental Drivers: Humid tropical climate creates high demand for regular hair care and scalp-cleansing services.
  • Competitive Environment: Abundant traditional hair salons and massage shops, but zero premium scalp-wellness specialized head spas.
  • Licensing: Operates under standard salon guidelines, facilitating quick establishment.

10. Customer Journey

  1. Booking: Online via Instagram/Whatsapp with automated slot booking.
  2. Welcome: Arrive in our cozy botanical lobby, served warm ginger tea.
  3. Analysis: Microscope check displays scalp health to the customer.
  4. Treatment: Transition to private starlight VIP pod for carbonated therapy.
  5. Blowdry & Close: Post-treatment comparison scan, blowout styling, package promotion.

11. Setup & Layout

The 800 sq ft space is optimized for visual privacy and client flow:

  • Lobby & Consultation (150 sqft): Coastal botanical themes, retail display shelves.
  • 4 Private VIP Pods (400 sqft): Soundproofed walls, starlight projection ceilings, Japanese wash beds.
  • Blowdry Station (150 sqft): Compact styling chairs and professional vanity mirrors.
  • Storage & Utility (100 sqft): Carbonated water system generators, laundry, organic stock.

12. Staffing Model

Stylist and therapist salary benchmarks in Kuching support an efficient operations budget:

  • 1 Store Manager: USD 1,250/month base salary (manages bookings, guest relations, accounting).
  • 4 Therapists: USD 750/month base salary (trained in specialized Japanese head spa techniques).
  • 1 Assistant: USD 500/month (handles laundry, cleaning).
Commissions: 10% commission sharing on membership package sales to motivate therapists and maximize retention.
